About01/About

Linux and AI are the two things I keep coming back to. I like the why behind them: the logic of a system, the reasons for a design decision, how an architecture fits together. I’m not tied to one distro, model or tool; I’d rather understand what’s underneath. I learn most by breaking things and fixing them.

I run models locally and build small tools with coding agents: automations, fixes for my own setup, things that save me time. I also use AI for research and to keep up with new technology. I like owning my tools and my data, so I lean toward local, open source software. When a fix belongs upstream, I try to send it there.
