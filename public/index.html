FAQ

Good questions.

Do you work with clients across the U.S.?

Yes. Everything is done remotely, and calls are booked during U.S. business hours, at a time that suits you.

Can you work with the tools I already use?

Yes. We start from what you have, like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or your current website, and I’ll only suggest a change when it’s clearly worth it.

How much does a project cost?

Every project and its starting price are on the prices page, and your plan gives the fixed figure before anything starts.

Why a fixed price instead of an hourly rate?

So you know the total before anything starts. If a job takes longer than planned, the price stays the same.

How do payments work?

Half when you approve the plan, and the other half on delivery, in U.S. dollars. Payments go through Upwork, which holds each one safely until you approve the work and adds a small fee of its own. You can pay by card, PayPal or U.S. bank transfer.

Is there a monthly fee?

Only if you want one. Projects are paid once, and an optional care plan keeps an eye on everything afterwards, for $69 a month.

Can you work to a deadline?

Yes. Mention the date in your first message, and the plan is built around it.

Do I need to share my passwords with you?

Only when a service offers no other way. Most let you add me as a user with just the access the job needs, so your password stays yours. When one doesn’t, you send me that one login in an encrypted link that expires, never by email, and change the password once we’re done.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. If you’d like one, I’ll sign it before we go into details.

Do you work with agencies and other freelancers?

Yes. I join agencies, designers and developers on projects that need web, email or AI work.
