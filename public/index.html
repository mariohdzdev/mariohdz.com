01/About

I help small businesses, independent professionals and U.S. companies get the technology behind their work right.

For more than ten years I’ve worked in customer service, technical support and operations, the last five remotely with U.S. companies. That’s where I learned to listen first, explain technical things in plain words and stay with a problem until it’s actually solved.

Linux and AI are what I keep coming back to. I spend part of every day researching new tools, models and updates, and testing which ones are worth using. I like understanding why things work, and I learn most by breaking them and fixing them.

I like owning my tools and my data, and I set things up the same way for the people I work with: in their name, on software they can trust, with nothing they don’t need.

02/Services

The technology behind your work, set up properly.

  • 02.1

    Web, domains and email

    A professional presence on a domain you own, with email that lands in the inbox.

    What’s included
    • Fast, simple websites, designed, built and published for you
    • Business email like you@yourcompany.com, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC
    • Domains, DNS and redirects on Cloudflare, set up cleanly
  • 02.2

    AI and automation

    AI that fits the way you work, and fewer hours lost to repetitive tasks.

    What’s included
    • AI assistants set up around your documents, instructions and tools
    • Automations and small tools, like turning PDFs into spreadsheets or pulling data from an API
    • Local, private AI that keeps your files on your own computer
  • 02.3

    Computers, accounts and privacy

    Computers that behave and accounts you control.

    What’s included
    • Linux desktop setup, moves from other systems and troubleshooting

03/Process

How we’ll work together

  1. 01

    Understand

    We start with what you need and why. Sometimes the right fix is smaller than it looks.

  2. 02

    Research

    I compare the options before choosing a tool or an approach, so you get what fits your case, not just what’s popular.

  3. 03

    Build

    I do the work carefully and test it before you see it. AI is one of my tools, not a shortcut, and the judgment behind every decision is mine.
