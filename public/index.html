About01/About

Linux and AI are the two things I keep coming back to. I like the why behind them: how a system is put together and why it was designed that way. I’m not tied to one distro, model or tool. I’d rather understand what’s underneath, and I learn most by breaking things and fixing them.

I spend part of every day researching new tools, models and updates, and testing which ones are worth keeping. I run models locally and build small tools with coding agents: automations, fixes for my own setup, things that save me time.

I like owning my tools and my data, so I lean toward local, open source software. When I fix something in an open source project, I try to send the fix back so everyone benefits.

I’ve also spent more than ten years in customer service, technical support and operations, the last five working remotely with U.S. companies. That’s where I learned to explain technical things in plain words.
