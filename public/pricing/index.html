FAQ

Questions about prices.

Why do prices say “from”?

Because every business is a little different. Each price covers the smallest version of its project, and your plan gives the fixed figure before anything starts.

What makes a project cost more?

Its size: more pages, mailboxes, people or connected apps. Each card says what its price covers, and the extras have their own prices.

What isn’t included?

Subscriptions, like your domain, email or AI plan, which you pay directly, in your name. And new work after delivery, which gets its own fixed price.

How do payments work?

Projects are billed in U.S. dollars, half to start and half on delivery, by U.S. bank transfer (ACH or wire) or by card through a secure link, with no account to create. The care plan renews each month by card, and you can cancel it any time.
