FAQ

Questions about AI and automation.

Is my data safe with an AI assistant?

Business plans of tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot don’t use your data to train their models by default. For files that must never leave your office, private AI runs on your own computer.

Which AI tool will you use?

The one that fits your work and your budget, often the one your team already pays for. I follow new models closely and test each one before recommending it.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Everything is set up for you, and your team learns to use it in plain words.

What if an app changes and my automation stops?

Fixes in the first 30 days are included. After that, the care plan keeps everything I set up working, or the fix gets a fixed price like any other job.
