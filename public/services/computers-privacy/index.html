FAQ

Questions about computers and privacy.

Will my programs work on Linux?

Most will, and the rest usually have a good alternative. We check the ones you rely on before anything changes.

Can this be done remotely?

Yes. Most of the work happens in a remote session, and the few steps that need hands on the computer we do together on a call.

Do I have to leave Google or Microsoft completely?

No. You can move only what you want, at your own pace, and keep the rest as it is.

Can you fix a problem on a Linux computer I already have?

Yes. Tell me what’s happening, and you’ll get a fixed price before anything starts.
