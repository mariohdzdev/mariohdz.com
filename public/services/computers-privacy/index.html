    Why it matters

    Linux runs fast without ads or tracking, gets security updates for years and often gives an older computer a second life. For most people the change is smaller than they expect, because their browser and files work the same.

    Why it matters

    Most break-ins start with a reused or stolen password. A password manager gives every account its own strong one, and two-step sign-in means a password alone can’t open the door. When someone leaves, their access leaves with them.

FAQ

Questions about computers and privacy.

Will my programs work on Linux?

Most will, and the rest usually have a good alternative. We check the ones you rely on before anything changes.

Can this be done remotely?

Yes. Most of the work happens in a remote session. For the few steps that need hands on the computer, like starting it from a USB drive, you get short, clear instructions.

Can you fix a problem on a Linux computer I already have?

Yes. Tell me what’s happening, and you’ll get a fixed price before anything starts.
