Why it matters

Why people switch.

Linux runs most of the world’s servers, and on a desktop it has become simple enough for everyday use. People move to it for three reasons.

  • Speed

    A second life for your computer.

    Linux asks less of your hardware, so many computers that feel slow today run smoothly again.

  • Privacy

    No ads, no tracking.

    There’s no advertising account behind the system, and you decide what, if anything, it shares.

  • Updates

    Security for years.

    Updates arrive for free, for years, without forcing a restart in the middle of your work.

FAQ

Questions about switching to Linux.

Will my programs work on Linux?

Most will, and the rest usually have a good alternative. We check the ones you rely on before anything changes.

Can I go back to Windows or macOS?

Yes. Your files are copied before anything changes, so they’re safe whichever system you use.

Can this be done remotely?

Yes. Most of the work happens in a remote session. For the few steps that need hands on the computer, like starting it from a USB drive, you get short, clear instructions.

Which Linux will you install?

One that’s stable, widely used and easy for everyday work, chosen for your computer and the way you use it.

Can you fix a problem on a Linux computer I already have?

Yes. Tell me what’s happening, and you’ll get a fixed price before anything starts.
