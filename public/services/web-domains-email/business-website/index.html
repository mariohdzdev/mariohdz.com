Signs

You probably need this if…

  • Your current site looks dated, loads slowly or breaks on phones.
  • Clients keep asking things your website should already answer.

Why it matters

What a good website does for you.

A website isn’t a brochure. It’s where a stranger decides whether to trust you, usually in less than a minute and usually on a phone.

  • Clarity

    It answers before they ask.

    Each service gets its own page that says what it is, who it’s for and what happens next, so clients arrive at the first conversation already informed.

  • Speed

    It loads before they leave.

    A fast page keeps visitors, and Google takes speed and how a page works on phones into account when it ranks results.

  • Ownership

    It stays yours.

    Domain, hosting, code and analytics are in your name from day one, so you’re never tied to one provider.

One page per service.

Clients find what they need faster, each page can show up for its own searches, and adding a service later is as simple as adding a page.

Process

How it’s built.

  1. 01

    Plan

  2. 02

    Write

    The text is written with you from your notes, in your clients’ words, so every page has a clear job.

    Launch

    The site goes live in your name and is sent to Google and Bing, and old addresses point to their new pages.

FAQ

Questions about a website for your business.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. We keep what already works and your web address, and every old page points to its new one, so visitors and search engines find you as before.

Do I need to write the text?

No. Send notes or documents you already have, and they become clear text that you review before anything goes live.

What if I need more than five pages?

Each extra page is $375, and your plan says exactly which pages are included.

Can I update the text myself?

Not with an editor: your site is hand-built to stay fast, so text changes come to me, each with a fixed price. The code is yours, so any developer can work on it too.

Will it work with my booking or payment tool?

Usually, yes. Most tools offer a link or a small piece of code to place on your site, and we check yours while planning.
