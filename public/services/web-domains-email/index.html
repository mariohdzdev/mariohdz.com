FAQ

Questions about websites and email.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. We keep what already works and your web address, and every old page points to its new one, so visitors and search engines find you as before.

Can I update the text myself?

Not with an editor: your site is hand-built to stay fast, so text changes come to me, each with a fixed price. The code is yours, so any developer can work on it too.

How long until my site shows up on Google?

Your site is submitted to Google and Bing as soon as it’s published, so it can appear within days. Ranking well takes longer and depends on your content and your competition.
