Signs

It’s for you if…

  • You’re starting a business, or making one you already run official.
  • You still write to clients from a Gmail or Hotmail address.
  • Your business has no website yet, or only a social media page.
  • Clients can’t find you on Google Maps.
  • You’d rather have one person set it all up than juggle several providers.

Why it matters

What clients see first.

Before anyone hires you, they look you up. In a few seconds they check three things: whether you have a website, whether your email comes from your own name and whether you show up on Google. Each one says your business is real and here to stay.

  • Domain

    An address that’s yours.

    Your domain is the name your website and email live on. Registered in your name, it stays yours whatever provider you use.

  • Website

    A page that answers first.

    One clear page says what you do, for whom and how to reach you, and turns visits into messages while you work.

  • Email

    Email that comes from you.

    An address like you@yourcompany.com, protected with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, reaches the inbox and can’t be easily faked.

And if you serve clients in one area, a Google Business Profile puts you on Google Maps and in local searches, often above every website.

Start small, but start right.

One page done properly does more than five rushed ones. You can add pages any time, and your domain and email stay the same.

Process

How it comes together.

FAQ

Questions about the launch package.

Do I have to choose my domain first?

No. We choose it together while planning, and you’ll know which options are free and which are worth it.

Can I grow to more pages later?

Yes. Start with one page and add more whenever you need them. With up to five pages from the start, the package costs $1,500 more.

Who writes the text?

You send a few notes about your business, and they become clear text for your page, which you review before anything goes live.

What will I pay each month after launch?

Your email plan, per person each month, and your domain, about $10–15 a year. Hosting for a page like this is free or a few dollars a month.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

Only if you serve clients in a specific area, like a salon, a clinic or a contractor. For a business that works only online, the website and email are enough.
