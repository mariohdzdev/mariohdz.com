A one-page website

One clear, fast page that says who you are, what you do and how to reach you, made for businesses and professionals who need clients more than pages.

From $1,500 · Ready in 1 to 2 weeks

Start this project How it works

Signs

It’s for you if…

  • Most of your clients find you through referrals or word of mouth.
  • People ask for your website, and you send them a social media profile.
  • Your current page is slow, outdated or hard to read on a phone.
  • You want something you’re proud to share, without a big project.
  • You need a place to send people from your email signature or your cards.

Why it matters

Why one page is often enough.

Most people who visit your site have already heard about you. They want to confirm three things quickly, and one well-made page answers all of them.

  • Who

    You, in plain words.

    A short statement of what you do and for whom, with your photo, so visitors know they’re in the right place.

  • Proof

    A reason to trust you.

    Your experience, a few results or a client’s words, placed where a doubtful visitor will see them.

  • Next step

    One obvious way to reach you.

    A clear button and a form that sends straight to your inbox.

Put your main button at the top, and again at the end.

Nobody should have to look for how to hire you. The same button in two places turns more visits into messages.

FAQ

Questions about a one-page website.

Is one page enough for Google?

For searches with your name and your service, yes. To show up for many different services, give each one its own page, which you can add later.

Can it grow into a bigger site later?

Yes. The same design takes more pages, at $375 each, and your domain and address stay the same.

Do I need a domain already?

No. We choose it together, and it’s registered in your name for about $10–15 a year.

Can it be in English and Spanish?

Yes. A Spanish version of the page is $300 more, written in natural Spanish.

Can I update the text myself?

Not with an editor: your site is hand-built to stay fast, so text changes come to me, each with a fixed price. The code is yours, so any developer can work on it too.
